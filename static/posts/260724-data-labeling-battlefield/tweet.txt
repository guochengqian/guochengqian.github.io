Title: Half of DeepSeek's core researchers are labeling data

The most surprising line from Liang Wenfeng: right now half of DeepSeek's people — including half its most important researchers — are labeling data.

Why it's the battlefield: at this stage (post-training), what actually moves model capability is high-quality labeled data. Not a bigger cluster — better labels.

Why China has no cost edge: high-end labeling isn't cheap manual work, it's expert judgment. On that, China's cost is no lower than the US's, and it's expensive either way. The labor-cost advantage that exists for low-end labeling just doesn't transfer.

How they close the gap: walk on two legs — label the cheap stuff first. And accept that the bottleneck is time, not money: even without more capital, existing capital already funds the fastest possible ramp; the US just started earlier. His call: within a year, domestic high-quality data should catch up. Longer term, continual learning is what frees them from human labeling.
