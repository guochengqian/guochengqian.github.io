# Twitter post: QQQ vs SPY DCA backtest

**Tweet (attach chart.png):**

Should you DCA into QQQ or SPY? I backtested $10k/month using real dividend-adjusted data, 2000–2026.

QQQ won at every start year (2000/05/10/15/20): IRR 19.3%/yr vs 14.5%, final wealth 1.2–2.0x of SPY.

The catch: the entire edge comes from post-2010. In 2000–2009 QQQ did -6.9%/yr vs SPY's -1.1%.
