Title: 1GW went from impossible to table stakes

1GW went from impossible to table stakes.

2024: no datacenter on Earth ran at a gigawatt. 2026: five switch on, one per hyperscaler.

Who controls the compute now — H100e performance plus H100-equivalent facility load at 600k H100e/GW:

⚡ Current (2026):

Google — 4.04–6.13M · 6.7–10.2 GW

Microsoft — 2.62–4.43M · 4.4–7.4 GW

Amazon — 2.01–3.00M · 3.4–5.0 GW

Meta — 1.77–3.00M · 3.0–5.0 GW

Oracle — 0.85–1.56M · 1.4–2.6 GW

CoreWeave — 0.79–0.88M · 1.3–1.5 GW

xAI — ~1.39M · ~2.3 GW

⏳ Once built (targets, not live):

Meta (end-2026) — ~4M · ~6.7 GW

OpenAI Stargate — ~6M · 10 GW

OpenAI is no longer a row: Epoch attributes rented cloud compute to Microsoft, Oracle and CoreWeave. Google/Amazon include TPU/Trainium, so H100e is not a physical-card count.

The GW column is not actual meter load. It asks how much facility power an all-H100 fleet would need for the same peak performance, using ~600k physical H100s/GW.

xAI shows why H100e ≠ cards: ~1.39M H100e converts to ~2.3GW of H100-equivalent facility load, while its mixed modern fleet is estimated at ~1.29GW IT. “Nearly 2GW” is the planned supply target.
